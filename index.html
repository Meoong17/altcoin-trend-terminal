How to use this terminal

This terminal helps you prioritize which altcoins to research further — it does not make buy/sell decisions for you. Use the workflow below.

1. Scan the macro bar first

Before looking at individual coins, check the top bar: GLF (global liquidity), Repo Stress, and Market Regime. Altcoin longs historically work best when regime is BULL TREND with GLF above 60. If GLF is below 40 or regime is CRISIS, prioritize capital preservation over picking longs.

2. Filter coins by entry timing and fundamentals

Use the sort headers to rank by Entry Timing grade (A+ to Avoid) or Fundamental / VaF. A practical filter sequence:

• Entry Timing A+ or A — the current setup is worth a look.
• VaF ≥ 105 / F-Score (protocol tokens) — strong measured value capture.
• The technical setup readout is DISPLAY ONLY — NOT validated (momentum & technicals failed the cross-era test), so use it as context, not a filter.

Coins that pass the fundamentals + timing filters are worth deeper research. Avoid-grade entries are not actionable right now.

3. Understand the technical readout's building blocks

Click any coin row or hover the dotted labels. The technical readout (display-only, not validated) has drivers — the small chips under the number show which components (flow rotation, participation/volume, relative strength vs BTC, compression, plus a small confirmation filter) are pulling it up or down. The four core components: flow rotation (the share of volume that is aggressive buying — who is pressing, distinct from volume itself), participation (is activity building or drying up?), relative strength vs BTC, and volatility compression. Volume tells you there is activity; flow tells you who is driving it and which way. Use this as context to match the price story to your own thesis — not as a buy signal.

4. Use entry timing to decide when

Entry Timing (A+ to Avoid) tells you if now is a reasonable window, based on chart structure, relative strength and execution quality. It is separate from fundamentals. An A+ entry on a moderate technical readout can be an early setup — but since technicals are not a validated predictor, weigh entry timing against the fundamentals/VaF view, not against the technical number alone.

5. Check VaF for DeFi & infra coins

For coins with a VaF row (protocol tokens): VaF ≥ 105 (out of 150) indicates strong token-level value capture. Look at the PQ (protocol quality) vs NF (value capture) split — a high PQ with low NF means the protocol is good but the token doesn't capture value. The CONF grade (A–D) tells you how reliable the research is; machine-only rows cap at B.

6. Volume tells you if the market agrees

The 14-bar volume chart and ratio tape show participation. >2× the 7-day average = a spike (breakout, news, or capitulation). <0.5× = drying up. A trending coin with rising volume is more credible than one with fading volume. "Building" volume over 3 days vs 7 is a positive early signal.

7. The Altcoin Season Index for market timing

Above 75 = altcoin season (broad participation). Below 25 = bitcoin season — altcoin picks are harder to find. When the index is between 25–75, selective picking works. This measures breadth, not magnitude — a few winners can't fake it.

8. Cross-check with the fundamentals view

F-Score is the fundamentals-only view for protocol tokens (TVL growth, revenue yield on market cap, fee acceleration, value accrual to holders). VaF is the value-accrual framework (protocol quality + value capture + repricing). Both are measured-data views still pending the ≥60-day walk-forward test — treat them as research, not settled predictions. There is no 60/40 technical+fundamental composite here because the technical leg was rejected empirically.
